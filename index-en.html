Queers houses

A space to find queer flatshares in Brussels.

This site is a grassroots initiative aimed at making it easier for queer people to find safer housing. Here, you can list a place you have to share or let people know you're looking for one.

About this site

  • This site can only be found by people who have its URL. The idea is to keep a certain level of privacy, limit access to potentially malicious users, and build a housing-search community.
  • The site is a work in progress. If you have ideas to improve it, you can share them via the email at the bottom of the page.
  • For now, I will post the ads in the language in which they have been sent.
  • How it works is simple: one page to post your listing via a form, and one page where listings appear. Publishing is not automated yet — I receive the forms by email and add listings manually when I have time.

Site rules

  • Use the pronouns and name the person has indicated in their listing. If you're not sure, ask.
  • If a listing seems problematic to you, you can report it via the address at the bottom of the page.

Who's behind this site?

Someone just like you, struggling to find a flatshare. Someone with no computer experience who decided to start this project anyway. This site is an activist, non-commercial, independent project. No data is sold or shared.
