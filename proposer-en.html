List a flatshare

Whether you have a place to share or you're looking for future flatmates, fill in this form. Your listing will be visible on the "Browse listings" page.

Tip: do not indicate the street and number of the shared accommodation. You can provide this information to interested parties when they contact you.
For your security, we recommend that you provide a dedicated contact method (e.g., Signal number, Proton email) rather than your primary contact information. This contact will be publicly visible on the listing.

To avoid going through a third party, the form will open in your email application when you click on "send the announcement". If your browser displays a security warning, that’s normal: the form simply opens your email application. Your data does not pass through an intermediary server. And if the form does not open in your email application, you can also write to us directly at mercilacolere@proton.me with the same information.
